Tie a Barrel Knot on the turkey with food safe twine:

  1. Set the turkey vertically on its neck end with the legs pointing up.
  2. Lay your rope across the top of the bird and tie a loose overhand knot.
  3. Pull the loop open and slide it down the sides of the bird so it cradles the turkey just under the wings.
  4. Pull the ends of the rope tightly upward so the loop constricts around the body, trapping the wings tightly against the sides.
  5. Tie a secure knot at the top and form a hanging loop.
