  • Troop 2 Advancement Coordinator:
    • General Role Description:
      • Record keeping and processing for all awards the Scouts Earn
    • Key Responsibilities:
      1. Coordinate and Chair all Boards of Review
        • Participate in Eagle Boards of Review, but currently CC Coordinates with District
        • 1-4, once a month ~20 min each
      2. Maintain inventory of rank patches for immediate recognition (by Unit Leader) the night of the BoR
        • Each CoH Ordering cycle - time is part of Purchase item
      3. Ensure Rank Dates are entered after completion of Boards of Review
        • Each BoR - time is part of BoR item
      4. Work with Unit Leaders to ensure approvals of records in ScoutBook Plus prior to processing for CoH
        • Each CoH Ordering cycle - time is part of CoH item
      5. Use ScoutBook Plus to generate list of items to purchase for CoH
        • Each CoH Ordering cycle - time is part of CoH item
      6. Purchase and prepare for CoH the appropriate awards
        • Each CoH Ordering cycle ~ 3 hours per CoH
      7. Provide a list of Awards to person createing the CoH Program
        • Each CoH Ordering cycle ~ 1 hours per CoH
      8. Use ScoutBook Plus to generate reports as needed (ie, Eagle Scout Rank Application)
        • varries based on need, but Committee Meeting gets 1 Report which takes about 15 min to generate and prep
      9. Work with Unit Leaders to ensure proper managment of records in ScoutBook Plus (not really an Advancement role...)
        • completely varriable
    • Time nets out to an avarage of 2-10 hours per month
  • Venture Crew Advisor:
    • General Role Description:
      • Work with Venture Crew President (and other officers) to facilitate the Venture Crew President running the Crew as described in Venture Crew Advisor training.
    • Key Responsibilities:
      • Safety of Venture Crew members
      • Advise Venture Crew President - this should be done with the President before any meeting/event (following SGY!), not during
    • Approximate time commitment:
      • Attend all Venture Crew activities
        • Meetings are for 1 hour First and Third Tuesdays before the Troop meetings
        • The Crew tries to have a Crew-only event at least 4 times a year (ie, evening of bowling, or weekend campout)
        • The Crew frequently attends Troop events, etc
        • Crew High Adventure early summer each year (typically 5-15 days)
